Marie Van Den Bogaard

Associate Professor in Computer Science
Université Gustave Eiffel · LIGM

I am an Associate Professor (Maîtresse de conférences) in Computer Science at Université Gustave Eiffel, where I am a member of the LIGM.

My research lies at the intersection of formal methods and algorithmic game theory. I am interested in different notions of rational behaviour in multiplayer games, and in the algorithmic questions arising from these notions, particularly in infinite-duration games on graphs.

For the academic year 2026–2027, I am on a one-year CNRS research leave (délégation) at the Laboratoire Méthodes Formelles (LMF), Université Paris-Saclay.
